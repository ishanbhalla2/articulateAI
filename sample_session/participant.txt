The architecture is, um, fairly simple. Documents land in a Unity Catalog volume. A pipeline chunks them and creates embeddings, and those go into a vector search index. When a question comes in, we retrieve the top five chunks and pass them to the model with the question. [[slnc 1000]]
For governance, everything sits in Unity Catalog, so, you know, the assistant can only see documents the user is allowed to see. [[slnc 800]]
